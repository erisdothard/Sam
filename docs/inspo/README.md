## What this board says about Sam's site
1. **Show the loop being built.** Not one looper on the board puts the act on its home page. Sam
   already has three phone videos of exactly that. They are the hero, played where they sit.
2. **The site is his studio room.** Khruangbin builds a room out of window light; Sam has a real one:
   snow in the windows, a pine floor, a red kilim, a sunburst guitar, a gold sax, a loop station with
   green-lit track rings. The palette and the one signature come from that room, nothing invented.
3. **Dated, owner-made, phone-native,** like Fred again's diary. Sam titles his videos by date
   ("Live loop 11/23/26"). The site keeps that log voice for videos and shows alike.
4. **Two doors,** like Cory Wong: Play and Teach, equal weight, on the home page.
5. **No popups, no cookie wall, no age gate, no pill buttons, no circles-and-rainbows lessons page.**
   Every reference here loses its first screen to one of those.
